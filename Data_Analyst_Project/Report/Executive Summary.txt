EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The analysis shows that revenue growth is not translating into profitability and
customer retention at the same rate.

Key issues are loss-making products, excessive discounting, 118 retention-risk customers,
weak Central and East regions, product returns, and inefficient Facebook marketing.

The main opportunities are to improve product profitability, retain valuable customers,
reduce returns, improve regional performance, and optimize marketing spending.

Overall, these actions can improve profitability, customer retention, and sustainable business growth.
